Who we are

上海爱沐莎国际贸易有限公司 — Lucia Trading Ltd., Shanghai

We are a wine import and distribution company headquartered in Shanghai. Our customers are restaurants, wine bars, hotels, regional distributors and online shops across China. We focus on characterful European wines — bottles with a story, a region and a family behind them — and we present them to Chinese consumers in their own language, through the channels they actually use.

What we do for our vineyards

A complete route into the Chinese market, handled by one partner.

Import & Compliance

Full import handling: shipping, customs clearance, Chinese back-label compliance and food-safety registration. Your wine arrives ready to sell, legally and on time.

Warehousing & Logistics

Climate-managed warehouses in Shanghai with regional stock locations, and same-week delivery to customers in major cities — from a single case to full pallets.

Sales & Distribution

An established B2B network: restaurants, wine bars, hotels and regional distributors. We place your wines on lists and shelves, not just in a catalogue.

Marketing, the Chinese Way

Chinese-language brand storytelling where it matters: WeChat official accounts, Xiaohongshu (RED), tastings and trade events — because in China, discovery happens on the phone.
